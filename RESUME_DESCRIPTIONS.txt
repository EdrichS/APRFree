APRFree: resume descriptions
============================

Three versions. Every number below is true of the project as it stands on October 5, 2026.
Keep the claims you can speak to in an interview, and adjust the verbs to match your own role.


1. SOFTWARE ENGINEERING
-----------------------
APRFree | iOS debt-payoff app | Swift, SwiftUI, Swift Charts, WidgetKit, App Intents

- Built a native iOS 26 app (about 7,000 lines of Swift across 15+ screens) that tracks credit
  cards, buy-now-pay-later plans and loans, splits each paycheck across them, and projects a
  debt-free date.
- Designed a payoff engine separate from the UI: a day-by-day interest simulation, four payoff
  strategies, bank-style due-date rules, and a full activity history with undo that replays later
  entries to keep them consistent.
- Wrote 42 automated tests, with expected values cross-checked against an independent Python
  model of the same rules.
- Added a configurable Home Screen widget, local notifications, CSV and PDF export, and
  backup and restore, with all data stored on the device and no network code.

One-line version:
Native SwiftUI debt-payoff app with a tested simulation engine, four payoff strategies, undoable
history and a configurable widget; all data stays on the device.


2. DATA SCIENCE
---------------
APRFree | Debt-payoff modeling and visualization | Swift, Swift Charts, Python

- Modeled multi-debt repayment as a day-by-day simulation (daily interest accrual, amortizing
  loans, promotional rates, fixed installment schedules) and compared four payoff strategies on
  total interest and payoff date.
- Validated the model two ways: an independent Python re-implementation that matches to the cent,
  and an exhaustive search over all 5,040 payoff orderings showing none beats highest-APR-first.
  On the sample portfolio, ranking by dollar interest instead cost about 60% more interest
  ($3,226.98 against $2,018.62).
- Built what-if analysis (one-time payments, monthly extras, new purchases, strategy changes) with
  interactive charts that read both scenarios' balances on any date.
- Designed the views that explain the numbers: balance breakdown by share and daily interest,
  per-card total cost on the plan versus minimum payments, and progress by debt category.

One-line version:
Simulation model of multi-debt repayment, validated against an independent implementation and an
exhaustive search of 5,040 orderings, with interactive what-if charts.
