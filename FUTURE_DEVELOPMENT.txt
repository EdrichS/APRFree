APRFree: future developments and implementations
================================================

Last updated: October 5, 2026


1. FINISH AND VERIFY WHAT EXISTS
--------------------------------
- Run on a physical iPhone. Set the signing team on both targets and confirm the app group works.
- Check the lock-screen widgets (rectangular and circular). Only small and medium have been seen running.
- Confirm reminders fire: due date the day before at 9:00, payday at 9:00.
- Exercise CSV export, PDF summary, Back Up Now and Restore end to end.
- View the onboarding strategy step with four options, and the new card and breakdown screens in dark mode.
- Add an app icon.
- Bank names are now text in typefaces that ship with iOS, not logos. Decide how far to keep that
  styling for the App Store (see preparation.txt, section 2).
- Put the project under version control.


2. OPEN DECISIONS
-----------------
- Highest Balance currently ranks by monthly interest in dollars (balance x APR / 12). Decide whether
  it should instead rank by raw balance, which is what the name suggests.
- Pay-later installments have no 9-day lock, so paying the next one early advances the plan. Decide
  whether they should follow the same lock as cards and loans.
- Due dates pushed too far by the old logic are not rolled back automatically. Either add a one-time
  repair or leave it to the "Next due date" field in the edit form.
- The design canvas still shows the original Progress, Simulator, card and widget layouts. Update it
  or retire it.
- More open decisions found in the October 5 review are listed in checkup_oct5.txt, section 5: the
  debt-free month, bonus money in Make a Payment, and minimums marked paid inside the 9-day lock.


3. FEATURES LEFT OUT OF VERSION 1 ON PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------
- Face ID lock.
- iCloud sync between devices.
- Card design customization.
- Statement check-in: a monthly prompt to enter the statement balance, so interest is captured.
- Links that open each bank's app from a split line.
- Balance transfer calculator.


4. MAKE THE MONEY MATH RICHER
-----------------------------
- Card interest. Balances only change when the user edits them or adds a charge. Options: statement
  check-in (above), or an optional estimate that accrues interest between check-ins.
- Minimum payments that follow the balance (for example 1% plus interest, with a floor) instead of
  one fixed number.
- Promo-aware strategy: pay a 0% card off just before its promo ends instead of right after.
- Variable APRs and a rate-change history.
- Late fees and annual fees as their own entry types.
- More than one income or payday schedule.
- A fifth strategy that minimizes the number of open accounts, and a "what should I do with $X today"
  quick answer outside of payday.
- Speed up the simulation. It walks day by day through the calendar; a closed form between paydays
  would make comparing thousands of orderings instant on the device.


5. HISTORY AND UNDO
-------------------
- Undo for deleting a debt.
- Redo, and a single "undo last action" shortcut.
- Edit an entry's amount or date instead of undoing and re-entering it.
- After an undo, only today's point on the Progress chart changes. Rebuild past points from the
  records so the chart matches.
- Filter and search the activity screen by debt, type and date.


6. INSIGHT AND REPORTING
------------------------
- Month-over-month and year-in-review summaries.
- Credit utilization over time, and the date each card drops under 30%.
- A richer PDF report with the charts.
- Export the plan itself (every future payday and where the money goes) to CSV or Calendar.


7. FORECASTING AND MACHINE LEARNING (IDEAS, NOT STARTED)
--------------------------------------------------------
The app has no machine learning today. If it grows in that direction:
- Forecast card spending from logged charges, so the plan accounts for new purchases.
- Flag unusual charges or a balance that moved more than the recorded activity explains.
- Suggest a safety buffer from the user's own payment history.
All of it would have to run on the device to keep the "everything stays on your iPhone" promise.


8. QUALITY AND REACH
--------------------
- UI tests and snapshot tests for each screen in light and dark mode.
- iPad and landscape layouts.
- Localization, and currencies other than US dollars.
- Include the independent Python check model in the repository so the test figures can be reproduced.
- Continuous integration that builds and runs the tests.
- App Store preparation: see preparation.txt.
