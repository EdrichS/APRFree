APRFree: checkup, October 5, 2026
=================================

What was checked, what was found, what was fixed, what was left alone, and what to do next.
Some of this repeats preparation.txt.


1. RESULT IN SHORT
------------------
- 51 automated tests pass (47 before today, 4 added). They also pass under the address,
  undefined-behaviour and thread sanitizers.
- The static analyzer reports nothing. Debug and Release builds have no warnings.
- No memory leaks found in two passes over the running app.
- 31 problems found and fixed, from gaps in how data is saved down to a line of text that wrapped.

3. WHAT WAS FOUND AND FIXED
---------------------------

3.1 Paydays (your request: check the predicted paydays, the 31st, a Friday, and so on)
- A payday that had passed restarted the count from "today", which changed the weekday. Paid every
  other Friday and opened on a Monday, the next payday became a Monday. Now the rhythm continues
  from the payday you gave: still a Friday.
- Monthly on the 31st drifted. After February the app paid on the 28th forever. It now remembers
  the day you are paid on: Jan 31, Feb 28 (Feb 29 in a leap year), Mar 31, Apr 30, May 31.
  The 29th and 30th work the same way.
- Weekly and every-two-weeks keep their weekday across month ends, year ends and both clock
  changes (tested 60 paydays ahead).
- "Twice a month" is removed; three options remain. Data saved with the old option still loads,
  as every two weeks.
- If the app stayed in memory overnight, it kept yesterday's plan. It now recalculates when it
  comes back to the front on a new day.
- Setup and Settings both show the next three paydays so the rhythm can be seen before saving.

3.2 No income entered (your request: the graph leaving its container)
- Cause: with no money going in, the plan never ended, so the projected balance kept growing and
  the line ran out of the top of the chart. The simulation also ran far longer than it needed to.
- Now: with nothing to pay with there is no plan line at all. Progress and the Simulator say "no
  payoff plan yet" and point to Settings. A plan whose payments never keep up with the interest
  stops being simulated after 26 paydays without progress. Charts are clipped to their tiles.
- Checked on screen with a 29.99% card and no income, on both Progress and the Simulator.

3.3 Protecting saved data
- If the saved file ever failed to load (a damaged file, or a future version changing the format),
  the app started empty and the next change saved over the real data. Now a file that cannot be
  understood is set aside as data-unreadable.json and never overwritten.
- iOS can start an app before the iPhone is unlocked, when the file cannot be read yet. The app
  would have shown setup and could then have saved over the data. Now it waits, loads the file when
  the app comes to the front, and saves nothing until it has.
- Numbers read from a backup are kept inside sane ranges, so a damaged or hand-edited backup
  cannot stall the app (tested with 2,000,000,000 installments and negative balances).
- Money and percent fields no longer accept negative or absurdly large values (possible before by
  pasting or with a hardware keyboard).

3.4 Dates
- My mistake, now corrected: the chart clipping I added earlier today for 3.2 also cut off the
  "Today" label and half of the last payoff dot on the Progress chart. The clip now covers the
  whole chart area instead of only the plot.
- Time zones. Dates were stored as "midnight where you were". After moving west, a due date read
  as the day before, and a minimum already met could show as due again. Saved files and backups
  now record their time zone and dates are moved to the new zone on loading, so Oct 9 stays Oct 9.
  The widget shows dates in the zone the app wrote them in.
- CSV export and the backup file name wrote dates in GMT, which is the day before for anyone east
  of GMT. They now use the local day.
- Chart labels on a short plan repeated the month ("Oct 26, Oct 26, Oct 26"), and "Oct 26" could
  be read as a day. Short charts now label days ("Oct 12"); long ones label "Oct '26".
- A date label at the very edge of a chart was cut off. That one label is now left out.

3.5 Due dates and forms
- A credit card saved with no minimum payment could never have its due date met, so it stayed
  overdue forever and reminders said "$0.00 minimum". The form now asks for a minimum when the
  card has a balance.
- A paid-off card or loan could not be edited at all: saving required a balance above zero. It
  can be edited now.
- A greyed-out Add or Save button gave no reason. A line above it now says what is missing.
- Editing a pay-later plan's other details reset money already paid toward the next installment.
  The balance is now kept unless the total or the payment counts change.
- New debts were stamped with the real clock instead of the app's clock.

3.6 Reminders and the widget
- Reminders and widget numbers were only refreshed when data changed. They are now refreshed
  every time the app opens and when the day changes.
- The reminder for the due date after next showed what was left of the current one (for example
  $10.00 after a part payment) instead of the full minimum.
- With many debts, the nearest reminders could be the ones dropped at iOS's limit of 64. The
  soonest are now scheduled first.
- The setup screen's example reminder always said "Chase is due tomorrow, due Oct 9". It now shows
  your own first debt and its real amount and date.
- Reset Everything also clears reminders already showing in Notification Center.

3.7 Money figures and export
- On a card's screen the sentence "Your plan saves $1,255.24" disagreed by a cent with the two
  figures above it ($1,270.02 and $14.77). The sentence and the Total interest row are now worked
  out from the figures as shown.
- The PDF summary silently dropped debts that did not fit on one page. It now continues onto more.
- A bank or card name beginning with =, +, - or @ would be run as a formula by a spreadsheet
  opening the CSV. Those are exported as plain text.
- The "extra each month" field read a comma decimal as thousands on iPhones set to regions that
  use a comma: typing 12,50 gave $1,250.00.
- Exported files waiting in the temporary folder are now protected while the iPhone is locked.

3.8 Small things
- Klarna's name was set too tightly. The credit card line in setup wrapped onto two lines.
- On Progress with no plan, the line under "Not yet" was cut off mid-sentence, and a chart with a
  single day of history was blank. The line is shorter now and the first day is marked with a dot.

3.9 Added for the App Store (not bugs)
- Privacy manifests for the app and the widget; the export-compliance key; a "not financial
  advice, not affiliated" note in Settings; widget balances marked private for the Lock Screen;
  and one switch (Wordmark.styled in Theme.swift) that turns all bank-name styling off.

5. LEFT AS IT IS: BEHAVIOUR YOU SHOULD DECIDE ON
------------------------------------------------
None of these is a crash or a wrong sum. Each is a rule that could reasonably go either way.
- "Highest Balance" ranks by monthly interest in dollars, not by raw balance.
- Pay-later installments have no 9-day lock; cards and loans do.
- The debt-free month is the month of the due date the last payment covers. Pay the last dollar on
  Nov 5 toward a Dec 1 due date and the app says December.
- Every full split in Make a Payment uses up the next payday. Splitting a bonus on the same day as
  a paycheck moves the next payday on by one more period.
- A minimum marked paid before its due date opens (inside the 9 days) is recorded as an extra
  payment and the due date stays. The split line does say "pay from" the date it will count.
- Card balances do not grow with interest on their own; they change when you edit them or add a
  charge.
- The monthly interest bars on a card's screen are each rounded to the cent, so they can add up to
  a cent more or less than the total beside them.
- After an undo, only today's point on the Progress chart changes; earlier points are not rebuilt.
- A new card's preview in the add form reads "PAID OFF $0.00" until a balance is typed.


6. NOT VERIFIED
---------------
- Anything on a real iPhone.
- Reminders actually firing. The scheduling code was read and corrected, not observed.
- The widget on screen after today's changes. Its numbers are tested; its drawing was only
  compiled. The Lock Screen sizes have never been seen running.
- CSV, PDF, Back Up Now and Restore through the share and file dialogs. Their contents are tested
  (CSV rows, PDF page counts, backup round trip), the dialogs were not driven by hand.
- The locked-iPhone start (3.3). It cannot be reproduced in the simulator; the logic was reviewed.
- The time-zone move by actually changing the device's zone. It is covered by a test.
- A comma-decimal region on screen.
- The "what is missing" line for loans and pay-later plans (seen for cards only).
- VoiceOver, large text sizes, small and large iPhones, and an iPad running the iPhone app.
- The last change of the day (the dot and the shorter line on an empty Progress chart, 3.8) was
  built and passed the tests but was not looked at on screen.
- The leaks tool noted that it can inspect a simulator app only in a limited way. Instruments on a
  real device is the fuller check.
- Speed was not measured formally. One plan takes roughly 12 ms in the simulator (720 plans in
  9 seconds in the test suite), so comparing four strategies is about a twentieth of a second.


7. SUGGESTIONS, MOST IMPORTANT FIRST
------------------------------------
1.  Put the project in git now. Today's changes touched saving and loading; there is no history to
    go back to if something turns out wrong.
2.  Run it on your own iPhone for a full pay cycle before anything else. Most of section 6 clears
    itself that way.
3.  Add the monthly statement check-in before a public release, so card balances track reality.
4.  Decide the items in section 5. Rename "Highest Balance" or change its rule; that one will
    confuse people first.
5.  Give Make a Payment a separate path for extra money that is not a paycheck.
6.  Keep a copy of a real saved file from every released version and add a test that each still
    loads. Saved data is the one thing an update must never break.
7.  Store dates as calendar days (year, month, day) instead of moments in time. Today's fix makes
    time zones safe; this would remove the whole class of problem.
8.  Add UI tests for setup, adding a debt, Make a Payment and Reset. Those flows were checked by
    hand today and will need checking again after every change.
9.  Add Face ID lock. It is the feature people will look for in an app showing what they owe.
10. For the App Store: icon, signing team, privacy policy page, and neutral demo data for the
    screenshots. See preparation.txt.
11. Put the independent Python check model in the repository so the expected figures in the tests
    can be reproduced by someone else.
