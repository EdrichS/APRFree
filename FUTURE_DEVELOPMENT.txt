APRFree: future developments and implementations
================================================

Last updated: October 6, 2026


0. DONE SINCE THE LAST VERSION OF THIS FILE (OCTOBER 6)
-------------------------------------------------------
Moved out of the lists below because they are built:
- Monthly statement check-in, on its own Statements page with a statement for every month.
- Credit utilization over time, and the date each card drops under 30%.
- More than one income, each with its own pay days, and a fixed monthly amount in their place.
- "What should I do with $X today": the Extra Money tool, and a separate path in Make a Payment
  for money that is not a paycheck.
- Undo for deleting a debt, redo, and a single "undo last action" shortcut with a confirmation.
- Editing an entry's amount or day.
- Past points on the Progress chart are rebuilt from the entries after an undo or an edit.
- A faster simulation that takes each stretch between paydays in one step.
- A PDF report with charts.
- Dates saved as calendar days instead of moments in time.
- A one-time repair for due dates the old logic pushed too far.
- The Highest Balance strategy was removed. Three remain.
- A privacy policy and a disclaimer that have to be accepted during setup.
- An eye button on the widget that hides the amounts.

2. OPEN DECISIONS
-----------------
- The debt-free month is the month of the due date the final payment covers, which can be the
  month after the last payment is actually made. Some people will expect the earlier month.
- In a paycheck split, a minimum whose due date is still inside the 9-day wait is recorded as an
  extra payment if it is marked paid early, and the due date stays. Decide whether marking it early
  should be blocked, or allowed and counted.
- The safety buffer is one amount held back from every paycheck, whichever income it is. With a
  small second income that may be too much. Consider a buffer per income.
- A fixed monthly amount is paid on one day of the month. Consider letting it be split across two.
- Insights estimates a month's interest; the statement shows only what was recorded. The two differ
  until check-ins are done every month (oct6check.txt, section 5). Make Insights use the recorded
  figure once a month has check-ins.
- The design canvas: see section 9.

Settled on October 6:
- Pay-later installments stay without the 9-day wait. Klarna's and Affirm's own rules were checked
  and the app already matches them (communicator.txt, section 6).


3. FEATURES LEFT OUT ON PURPOSE
-------------------------------
- Face ID lock.
- iCloud sync between devices.
- Card design customization.
- Balance transfer calculator.
- Anything that connects to a bank. The options, in order of what they cost, are in communicator.txt.


4. MAKE THE MONEY MATH RICHER
-----------------------------
- A monthly reminder for the statement check-in.
- Refuse, with a clear message, data saved by a newer version of the app.
- Card interest between check-ins: an optional estimate that accrues interest until the next
  statement check-in replaces it with the real figure.
- Minimum payments that follow the balance (for example 1% plus interest, with a floor) instead of
  one fixed number.
- Promo-aware strategy: pay a 0% card off just before its promo ends instead of right after.
- Variable APRs and a rate-change history.
- Late fees and annual fees as their own entry types.
- A fourth strategy that minimizes the number of open accounts.
- A "best order" finder. The plan now runs fast enough to try thousands of orders on the device,
  so the app could search for the cheapest order under the user's own constraints.
- Past credit limits. Utilization history is measured against today's limits.


5. HISTORY AND UNDO
-------------------
- Filter and search the activity screen by debt, type and date.
- Editing balance changes and statement check-ins. Today only payments and charges can be edited;
  the others can be undone and entered again.
- A longer undo history. The last 15 actions are kept.


6. INSIGHT AND REPORTING
------------------------
- Month-over-month and year-in-review summaries.
- A statement as a PDF of its own, one month at a time.
- Export the plan itself (every future payday and where the money goes) to CSV or Calendar.


7. FORECASTING AND MACHINE LEARNING (IDEAS, NOT STARTED)
--------------------------------------------------------
The app has no machine learning today. If it grows in that direction:
- Forecast card spending from logged charges, so the plan accounts for new purchases.
- Flag unusual charges or a balance that moved more than the recorded activity explains.
- Suggest a safety buffer from the user's own payment history.
All of it would have to run on the device to keep the "everything stays on your iPhone" promise.


8. QUALITY AND REACH
--------------------
- UI tests and snapshot tests for each screen in light and dark mode.
- iPad and landscape layouts.
- Localization, and currencies other than US dollars.
- Include the independent Python check model in the repository so the test figures can be reproduced.
- Continuous integration that builds and runs the tests.
- App Store preparation: see preparation.txt.


9. THE DESIGN CANVAS: OUT OF DATE, NOT UPDATED
----------------------------------------------
The Design canvas "APRFree v1 Screens" (https://claude.ai/artifact/7ubT4xqHRyFaBG5VBuRCbM) was
drawn before the app was built and has NOT been brought up to date. On October 6 it held 58 screen
artboards (29 screens, light and dark) plus a Logo-Icon artboard added after the originals. Updating
it means redrawing about two thirds of those by hand and adding eleven screens that do not exist
in it, and merging with the newer version already published to it. That is a job of its own.

Until it is done, treat the canvas as the original concept, not as a picture of the app. Either
update it in a session of its own, or retire it.

Artboards that no longer match the app:
- Onboarding 1b First Debt: now three "add" rows, an Added list, Done and Skip.
- Onboarding 1c Payday: three choices, and a line showing the next paydays.
- Onboarding 1e Strategy: three strategies, not two.
- Onboarding 1f Reminders: the example now uses the user's own debt.
- Main: bank names set as text, six tools instead of four, three page dots, Undo and Redo in the menu.
- Progress: switchable between Total, Credit cards and Loans; "Show All Activity".
- Card Detail (Chase, Citi): "Total Cost of This Card" in place of "Where Your Payment Goes"; a History row.
- Loan Detail: projected total interest in place of the small chart.
- Strategy: three strategies and the comparison table.
- Simulator: any custom amount; hold and drag to read the chart.
- Settings: no Payday section; Income, About and Reset Everything.
- Payment 7a Amount: the Paycheck / Extra money switch.
- Add Debt 6a Providers and the card form: bank names as text tiles; the line that says what is missing.
- Export: a PDF report rather than a one-page summary.
- Widgets: the total / credit cards / loans choice and the eye button.

Screens the app has that the canvas does not:
- Setup: Privacy Policy, Disclaimer.
- Statements (the stack of months), one Statement, the Statement Check-In sheet.
- Income, and the editor for one income.
- Extra Money ("Where It Goes").
- Balances (the ring chart), Activity (all of it), a debt's History, Edit Entry.

Still about right: Onboarding Welcome and Safety Buffer, Pick Card, the pay-later and loan forms,
the Klarna detail, Celebration, Home Empty, Insights, Payment Split and Mark Paid, Components.
